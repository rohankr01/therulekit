SECTION: 210.8(A)(1)
TITLE: GFCI Protection - Bathrooms (NEC Primary)
JURISDICTION: Los Angeles County, CA
YEAR: 2023
SOURCE_TYPE: raw_code

CONTENT:
All 125-volt, single-phase, 15- and 20-ampere receptacles installed in bathrooms shall have ground-fault circuit-interrupter protection for personnel. This requirement applies to receptacles located within bathroom areas of dwelling units and guest rooms. [NEC 210.8(A)(1)]

FIELD_TIP: GFCI protection is usually provided by either a GFCI receptacle or a GFCI circuit breaker in the panel.

INSPECTOR_FOCUS:
- Applies to dwelling unit bathrooms AND guest rooms
- All receptacles within bathroom boundaries

SECTION: 210.52(G)(1)
TITLE: Bathroom Basin Receptacle Location
JURISDICTION: Los Angeles County, CA
YEAR: 2023
SOURCE_TYPE: raw_code

CONTENT:
At least one receptacle outlet shall be installed in bathrooms and shall be located within 900 mm (3 ft) of the outside edge of each basin. The receptacle outlet shall be located on a wall or partition that is adjacent to the basin or basin countertop. [NEC 210.52(G)(1)]

FIELD_TIP: The receptacle cannot be installed inside cabinets or drawers.

INSPECTOR_FOCUS:
- Measure from OUTSIDE EDGE of basin
- Wall/partition must be adjacent to basin
- One per basin (double vanity = 2 receptacles)

COMMON_FAILURES:
- Inside vanity cabinet
- Over 3 ft from basin edge
- Back wall installation (not adjacent)

SECTION: 210.11(C)(3)
TITLE: Dedicated 20A Bathroom Receptacle Circuit
JURISDICTION: Los Angeles County, CA
YEAR: 2023
SOURCE_TYPE: raw_code

CONTENT:
In addition to the number of branch circuits required by other parts of this section, at least one 20-ampere branch circuit shall be provided to supply bathroom receptacle outlets. This circuit shall have no other outlets. [NEC 210.11(C)(3)]

INSPECTOR_NOTE: Lighting outlets are not permitted on the bathroom receptacle circuit.

FIELD_TIPS:
- Dedicated circuit - no lights, fans, or other loads
- 20A breaker with 12 AWG conductors
- Serves receptacles only

SECTION: LA-BATHROOM-GFCI-ENFORCEMENT
TITLE: LA County Bathroom GFCI Specifics
JURISDICTION: Los Angeles County, CA
YEAR: 2023
SOURCE_TYPE: enhanced_guide

CONTENT:
LA County inspectors strictly enforce bathroom electrical requirements:

INSPECTOR_FOCUS:
1. GFCI test button verification (every receptacle)
2. Downstream protection testing  
3. Tamper-resistant + GFCI verification
4. Dedicated 20A receptacle circuit
5. Basin receptacle location (within 3 ft)
6. No lights on receptacle circuit

COMMON_FAILURES:
- GFCI breaker on lighting circuit
- Receptacle inside cabinetry
- 15A circuit for receptacles
- Non-TR receptacles
- Shared circuits (lights + receptacles)

FIELD_TIPS:
- Use TR GFCI receptacles ($25-35)
- Dedicated 20A #12 NM-B circuit
- Label panel: "BATH RECEPTACLES 20A"
- Test GFCI monthly
- Document circuit in permit package

COST_ANALYSIS:
- TR GFCI receptacles (2): $60
- Dedicated 20A circuit: $450-650
- Inspection failure rework: $600-1200

JURISDICTION_AMENDMENTS:
- LA County requires TR + GFCI (no exceptions)
- All bathroom receptacles (no appliance exceptions)
